## Part 3: Step-by-Step Platform Upload Instructions

---

### 💼 How to Upload to LinkedIn

1. **Prepare Your Media**:
   - Ideal video length: **2 to 4 minutes**.
   - Format: **MP4** or **MOV**, 1080p (1920x1080 horizontal or 1080x1080 square).
   - Bitrate: Standard H.264.

2. **Upload Process**:
   - Open [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com) on your desktop browser.
   - Click **Start a post** at the top of your feed.
   - Click the **Media / Video icon** (film strip / photo icon).
   - Select your video file.
   - Click **Select Thumbnail** on the preview screen and choose a clean, visually striking frame (e.g., showing the Fonia Sphere homepage with the glowing logo).
   - Click **Done**.
   - Paste the **LinkedIn Post Copy** from Part 2 above.
   - Review formatting: ensure blank lines separate paragraphs for readability.
   - Click **Post**.

3. **Engagement Optimization**:
   - **Best Time to Post**: Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday between **8:00 AM – 10:30 AM**.
   - **First 60 Minutes**: Stay active and reply to every comment immediately to boost algorithmic reach.

---

### 🐦 How to Upload to Twitter / X

1. **Prepare Your Media**:
   - If you have standard X (free account): Maximum video length is **2 minutes and 20 seconds (140 seconds)**. Keep the video under 2:15 to be safe, or post a 90-second teaser.
   - If you have X Premium: You can upload videos up to 1080p and longer duration.

2. **Posting the Thread**:
   - Open [x.com](https://x.com).
   - In the "What is happening?!" composer, paste **Tweet 1**.
   - Click the **Media (Gallery) icon** and attach your demo video.
   - Click the **"+" button** at the bottom right of the composer to add the second tweet.
   - Paste **Tweet 2** (Architecture).
   - Click **"+"** again and paste **Tweet 3** (Sandbox).
   - Click **"+"** again and paste **Tweet 4** (UI / Design).
   - Click **"+"** again and paste **Tweet 5** (Links & CTA).
   - Review the complete thread chain.
   - Click **Post all**.

3. **Post-Publishing Step**:
   - Once posted, open Tweet 1, click the three dots (`...`) in the top right, and click **Pin to your profile**. This keeps your best work visible to anyone visiting your handle!

---

### 📸 How to Upload to Instagram (Reel)

1. **Prepare Your Media**:
   - Format: **9:16 vertical video** (1080 x 1920 pixels).
   - If your screen recording is 16:9 widescreen, use a video editor (CapCut, Premiere, or Canva) to place the recording on a vertical 9:16 canvas with a dark background and a title header like *"Building a Social Media Platform from Scratch ⚡"*.
   - Duration: **60 to 90 seconds**.

2. **Upload Process**:
   - Open the **Instagram mobile app** on your phone.
   - Tap the **"+" (Create)** button at the bottom or top bar and choose **Reel**.
   - Select your edited 9:16 video.
   - Tap **Next**.
   - Tap **Edit Cover** -> Choose a crisp frame or upload a prepared cover image so your grid stays aesthetic.
   - Paste the **Instagram Caption** from Part 2.
   - In the **Tag Topics** section, select *Software, Science & Tech, Web Development*.
   - Under **Share to Feed**, ensure the toggle is ON so it appears on both your main profile grid and the Reels tab.
   - Tap **Share**.

3. **Bio Link Setup**:
   - Make sure your Instagram bio has a link to your portfolio or GitHub: `https://fonia-labs.vercel.app` or `https://github.com/ifidonabraham`.
